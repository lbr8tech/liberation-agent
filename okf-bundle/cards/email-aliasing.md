## Steps

### General

SimpleLogin or Addy.io — both offer mobile apps (Android, iOS) and browser extensions (Firefox, Chrome, Brave). Generate a unique alias for every new signup without visiting the dashboard.

## Cloud

SimpleLogin (owned by Proton, 10 aliases free, browser extension generates aliases in-place, supports anonymous replies)

Alternatives: Addy.io (open source, more generous free tier, catch-all on custom domain, PGP on all tiers including free)
