## Steps

### General

GrapheneOS (Pixel phones only, maximum security) or /e/OS (Murena, many devices including Fairphone, easier setup). Both are de-Googled Android with FOSS app store. /e/OS has an easy installer for Fairphone. This is the "get off Google" path for someone with a Fairphone.

## Guides

1. grapheneos.org (GrapheneOS: security-hardened Android for Pixel phones, sandboxed Google Play compatibility)
2. murena.com (Murena /e/OS: de-Googled Android with easy installer, supports Fairphone and many devices)
3. eylenburg.github.io/android_comparison.htm (Eylenburg: Android OS comparison table — 6 distributions including GrapheneOS, DivestOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS with security, Google integration, and feature analysis)
