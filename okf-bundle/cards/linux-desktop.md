## Steps

### General

Ubuntu (most popular, easiest transition), Fedora (cutting edge, stable), or Debian (rock solid, conservative). Migration: dual-boot first, then full switch. Most daily tasks work fine — browser, email, media. Challenges: Adobe software, some games, Microsoft Office.
