## Why

Notifications are used to keep you addicted and distracted. Remove them to gain better control of your focus and flow.

## Steps

### General

Go through every app and disable all notifications except direct messages. No install needed.

### Android

Settings → Apps → [app name] → Notifications → Disable. Or Settings → Notifications → [app] → toggle off. Keep only messaging apps (SMS, Signal, etc.) enabled. For Android 8.0+ (Oreo), use per-app notification channels to disable specific types (e.g. marketing) while keeping direct messages.

### iOS

Settings → Notifications → [app name] → Toggle off Allow Notifications. Or Settings → Notifications → disable per app. Keep only messaging apps enabled. iOS does not offer per-notification-type granularity like Android channels.
