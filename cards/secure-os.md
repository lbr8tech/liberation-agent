## Steps

### General

Tails OS (amnesic, boots from USB, leaves no trace on hardware, all traffic through Tor) or Qubes OS (compartmentalized, each activity in isolated VM, very high security but steep learning curve). For journalists, activists, people facing targeted surveillance.

## Guides

1. tails.net (Tails OS: amnesic live USB, all traffic through Tor, leaves no trace)
2. qubes-os.org (Qubes OS: compartmentalized security, each activity in isolated VM)
3. eylenburg.github.io/os_comparison.htm (Eylenburg: operating system comparison — 18 systems with security, privacy, and architecture analysis)
