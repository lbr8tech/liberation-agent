## Steps

### General

Tor Browser (bounces traffic through 3 relays, hides IP, resists fingerprinting). For high-threat scenarios: Tails OS (amnesic, boots from USB, leaves no trace) or Qubes OS (compartmentalized, isolates each activity in a separate VM).
