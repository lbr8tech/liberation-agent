## Steps

### Android

Gadgetbridge (F-Droid, replaces proprietary watch apps for many devices). Endurain/wger via web app.

### iOS

Endurain/wger via web app. Gadgetbridge not available on iOS.

### Desktop

Endurain/wger web UI.

## Self-Hosted

Endurain (cardio tracking, FOSS) + wger (workout tracking, FOSS) + Gadgetbridge (FOSS replacement for proprietary fitness watch apps)
