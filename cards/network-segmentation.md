## Steps

### General

Create separate network segments: main (phones, laptops), guest (visitors), IoT (smart devices, cameras, TVs). If your router supports VLANs or multiple SSIDs, use them. Block IoT devices from accessing your main network. This limits damage if a smart device is compromised.
