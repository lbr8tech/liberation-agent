## Steps

### General

DNS-level blocking of distracting apps/websites with timed unblock negotiation. Instead of per-device app blockers, block at the DNS level so it applies to all devices. Timed unblock allows scheduled access windows. Protocol: ask purpose, offer duration choices, confirm before executing, surface usage history.
