## Steps

### Android

DAVx5 (F-Droid, syncs CalDAV/CardDAV to Baikal/Nextcloud).

### iOS

iOS supports CalDAV/CardDAV natively (Settings → Accounts → Add CalDAV/CardDAV Account).

### Desktop

Thunderbird or any CalDAV/CardDAV client.

## Self-Hosted

Baikal (lightweight CalDAV/CardDAV server)

Alternatives: Nextcloud (full suite including calendar/contacts)

## Prerequisites

Server access + backup strategy before hosting contacts/calendar data.
