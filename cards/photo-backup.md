## Steps

### General

Ente (cloud, E2E encrypted — Android/iOS/desktop apps) or Immich (self-hosted — Android/iOS apps + web UI). Both are open source.

## Self-Hosted

Immich (self-hosted, full Google Photos replacement, ML face detection)

Alternatives: Ente (can also be self-hosted), PhotoPrism (lighter, less features)

## Cloud

Ente (E2E encrypted cloud photo backup, open source)

Alternatives: Proton Drive (encrypted, integrated with Proton ecosystem)
