## Steps

### General

Subscribe to blogs, news sites, and YouTube channels via RSS instead of visiting platforms. RSS readers pull content on your schedule, not the platform's. Use Miniflux/FreshRSS (self-hosted) or a hosted reader.

## Self-Hosted

Miniflux or FreshRSS (self-hosted RSS reader)
