## Steps

### General

Four escalating threat levels — apply the measures for your situation:

**Level 1 — Everyday essentials (also in Phase 0 cards):**
- Keep OS updated (security patches are meaningless if outdated)
- Long passwords (3 random objects, combined)
- Regular backups (so you can wipe fast)
- Turn off lockscreen message previews
- Remove data broker apps (PayPal, Snapchat, Google apps)
- Use privacy-focused messengers, mail, and alt OS if possible

**Level 2 — Risky places (airports, borders, protests):**
- Turn off voice assistant (can be used against you)
- Enable auto-lock (1-5 min timeout)
- Turn off biometrics (face/finger can be forced). Use lockdown mode (Android/Pixel) or press power+volume to temporarily disable biometrics
- Enable disappearing messages in Signal (30 days or less)
- Black background wallpaper (photos of loved ones or yourself link back to you)
- Turn off Location, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi when not actively needed
- Download offline maps (Organic Maps/OsmAnd)
- Log out of non-essential accounts

**Level 3 — Being targeted (journalist, dissident, stalking victim):**
- Carry a burner device you can discard
- Phone stays in airplane mode unless actively needed
- Faraday bag (blocks all signals when not in use)
- Set up a duress PIN (different from real PIN — triggers instant wipe)
- Reboot before entering any sensitive environment (puts phone in BFU — Before First Unlock — state, much harder to crack)

**Level 4 — Detained or arrested:**
- Input duress PIN to wipe device
- You can restore from backup later
- Know your rights: US — cannot be compelled to hand over password (5th Amendment), but CAN be compelled for biometrics. UK, India, France — can be compelled for both
- Know your location — legal rights vary by jurisdiction
- Shut up. Say nothing without a lawyer
