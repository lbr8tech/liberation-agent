## Steps

### Android

AntennaPod (FOSS, F-Droid, full RSS podcast client). Level 2 (self-hosted): PinePods + gpodder sync.

### iOS

AntennaPod not available. Use Apple Podcasts with RSS-only subscriptions (no Spotify). Or use a podcast RSS app like Pocket Casts (not FOSS but supports RSS).

### Desktop

PinePods (self-hosted, FOSS) or gpodder (desktop sync).
